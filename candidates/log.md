# Log

## 2026-08-14

Bundle created. 110 candidate practices derived from a retrospective of one
project's first 577 units of agent-driven work; see
[`retrospective.md`](retrospective.md) for the method and the evidence.

- Split into two buckets: `chug/` (14) for practices specific to how an
  agent-orchestration platform works within its own framework, and `general/`
  (96) for practices written to stand alone, grouped by scope. The general
  bucket names no particular project.
- Adopted the Open Knowledge Format v0.2 as the bundle format. Every concept
  carries `type: Blessed Practice`, `status: draft`, and the taxonomy —
  scope, altitude, portability, confidence — as namespaced `tags`.
- Nothing is adopted. Promotion is a review decision: `status: stable` plus a
  `verified` entry naming who accepted it and when.
